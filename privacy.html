2. What is GoddessGrounded for?

GoddessGrounded is a private application for women in relationships. You use it to:

  • Complete a self-assessment across six personal dimensions
  • Set a daily intention for yourself
  • Reflect on your needs and boundaries
  • Receive daily tips based on your personal profile
  • Track your Groundedness Score over time
Important: GoddessGrounded processes data about you — not about your partner or third parties. The app is designed to support your own self-awareness and wellbeing within your relationship.

3. What data do we process?

3.1 Your account data

Account data: email address, registration date, language preference.

Waitlist data: email address submitted via the waitlist form on goddessgrounded.app, used solely to notify you of the app launch.

3.2 Your personal data

Assessment data: your responses to the self-assessment across six dimensions — self-connection, self-awareness, naming needs, boundaries, identity, and presence.

Daily data: intentions set, reflections recorded, tips viewed and rated, check-in responses.

Usage data: login times, session length, feature usage, Groundedness Score history.

3.3 What we do not process

GoddessGrounded does not process data about your partner or other third parties. We do not process financial data (GoddessGrounded is free), health data as defined under Article 9 GDPR, or location data.

6. Who do we share your data with?

GoddessGrounded never sells your data to third parties. We share data only with processors who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database hosting and storage (EU servers)
  • Vercel — web application hosting
  • Resend — email notifications
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — AI processing for personalised tips and content

All processors are bound by data processing agreements and process data in accordance with GDPR. We may provide data to government authorities if legally required to do so.

GoddessGrounded does not use Stripe — the app is free and no payment data is processed.

7. Is data processed outside the EU?

Some of our processors are located outside the European Economic Area (EEA). In such cases, we ensure an adequate level of protection through the use of European Commission-approved standard contractual clauses (SCCs) and recognised certification schemes.

8. Security of your data

Technical measures: encryption of data at rest and in transit (HTTPS), Row Level Security ensuring each user can only access their own data, magic link authentication without stored passwords, and regular security audits.

Organisational measures: minimal data access, confidentiality obligations, and an incident procedure for data breaches in accordance with GDPR requirements.

In the event of a data breach likely to affect your rights and freedoms, we will notify you within 72 hours in accordance with Article 34 GDPR.

11. Future partner link with GoddessAlert

Future feature — not yet active In a future version of GoddessGrounded, users will have the option to voluntarily link their account with a GoddessAlert account. This link is always opt-in, requires explicit consent from both users, and can be revoked at any time. Only data that both users have explicitly agreed to share will be exchanged. This privacy policy will be updated before this feature is activated.

When the partner link becomes available, the following principles will apply:

  • Both users must give explicit, separate consent before any data is shared
  • Each user controls what they share — consent is granular, not all-or-nothing
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time, immediately stopping all data exchange
  • Only the minimum data necessary for the feature will be shared
  • Every data transfer will be logged for transparency and AVG compliance
