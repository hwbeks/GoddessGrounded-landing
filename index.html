What GoddessGrounded is

Not a self-help app.
A daily return to yourself.

GoddessGrounded doesn't tell you what to fix. It asks the questions you've stopped asking yourself. What do I need today? What did I feel but not say? Where did I make myself smaller?

It's built on an insight at the heart of modern relationship therapy: you don't have to lose yourself to love someone. But it's also true without a relationship in the picture — the connection with yourself is always the most important one.

Small daily moments. Not to become a better partner, a better mother, or a better colleague — but to become more you.

How it works

A few minutes.
Every day.

01
Check in with yourself

A daily question that asks how you feel — about yourself, your needs, your energy. You first. Always.

02
Name what you need

Notice what you needed but didn't ask for. A gentle prompt to say it — to yourself first, and then to whoever needs to hear it.

03
Set an intention

One thing you want to bring into the day — for yourself. Not for the relationship, not for the family. For you.

04
Stay yourself

Whether you're single, partnered, or somewhere in between — the most grounded version of you is the best thing you can offer the world.
